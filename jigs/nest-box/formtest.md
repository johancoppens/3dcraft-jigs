# Form test

A starling family has been nesting under our eaves for years, and last summer the roof was renewed, so I want to give
them a proper box on the barn wall. I bought one larch board for it: 20 mm thick, 240 mm wide and 2.2 metres long. I
would like to screw the box to the wall with a strip behind it. I would also like something to stop a woodpecker making
the hole bigger. What do I saw, how do I lay it out on the board, and how much of the board will I have left?

## Acceptance

- The entrance is a single round hole of 45 mm; the floor inside is 160 by 160 mm, and the box is 300 mm high inside at
  the back. The boards are 20 mm thick.
- Seven boards to saw: two sides, a back, a front, a floor, a roof and a batten. The widest board across the plank is
  230 mm, which leaves 10 mm to rip off.
- The plan of the plank exports to one SVG or DXF with the plank outline and those seven boards labelled in sawing
  order; the board set exports with the same boards and a round guard plate at true size.
- The form says the plank needs about 2 170 mm (2 166 mm with saw kerfs), so about 34 mm of the 2.2 m is left over; the
  box may hang between 2 and 5 metres up. The predator guard is a zinc plate 69 mm across. All assertions that apply
  pass.
- Refusal: the same box from a board 150 mm wide cannot be set (the form clamps the width up to the smallest
  width that fits, 230 mm at this thickness, or `param.set` refuses it with that range).
