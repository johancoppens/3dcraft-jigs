# Form test: strandbeest-leg

"I am building a small wind-walker for the garden, in the spirit of Theo Jansen's beach animals. I want to start with
one walking module: two legs on one crank axle, working against each other, so that one foot is always on the ground.
The axle should ride 200 mm above the floor. I have walnut and birch plywood that I measured at 3.8 mm, and a length of
4 mm brass rod with washers to match. My laser burns away about 0.15 mm, and I want the joints to turn freely without
rattling. Between the two frame plates I need 18 mm for the toothed pulley that will drive the axle. The legs should be
the light version, with the triangles opened up, and the bars about 14 mm wide. How long a step does it take for each
turn of the crank, and how high does the foot lift? I need the files for my laser and a list of the brass rod I have to
cut and how many washers I need."

## Acceptance

- Two legs, half a turn apart; hip height 200 mm (the axle above the ground); plywood 3.8 mm; 4 mm brass rod with M4
  washers; kerf 0.15 mm; a hole clearance between 0.15 and 0.25 mm; 18 mm of room for the drive; open triangles; bars
  14 mm.
- Crank radius about 31.5 mm. Step length (the foot's travel along the ground, relative to the leg) about 142.7 mm, step
  height about 47.2 mm, the foot in its stance for about 61.6 % of the turn, so one of the two feet is always down.
  Longest part about 152.1 mm (the foot triangle). Least gap between parts on one layer about 13.8 mm.
- Rod: pins A and E 19.1 mm (4 of them), pins D and F 14.3 mm (4), pin C 9.5 mm (2), the pivot rod 46.6 mm and the
  axle 35.2 mm; 234.4 mm of rod in all. Two M4 mounting bolts with a grip of 27.2 mm; three spacer tubes 18 mm long;
  44 washers. The pulley may have a radius of up to about 57.5 mm.
- Every assertion that applies passes.
- The birch sheet exports with 20 parts (two frame plates, two cranks, eight bars, eight spacer rings) and the walnut
  sheet with 4 (two hip triangles, two foot triangles); the holes are drawn at 4.05 mm, 0.15 mm under the rod plus the
  clearance.
- Refusal: the same module from 6 mm plywood is refused, since a hole must be at least as wide as the sheet is thick
  (with 4 mm rod and 0.2 mm clearance the form stops at 4.2 mm). A hip height of 80 mm is refused too (the least at
  these settings is 133 mm): the bars could not keep their web round the holes and still pass each other.
