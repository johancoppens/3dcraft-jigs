# Form test

We are putting a small server cupboard on the wall of a concrete plant room. Twelve rack units of gear in 19 inch width,
the deepest box is 350 mm from its rack ears to the back of its connectors, and all the gear together weighs about
45 kg. I want a 2 mm steel cabinet on the wall, a door that hangs on the right so it opens away from the corner, with
vents, four M25 cable glands top and bottom, rear rails for the deep box and three blanking panels in the unused units.
How big is it, what does the box itself weigh, how much does each wall fixing have to hold, and what do I send to the
laser? And could I hang 200 kg of gear in the same cabinet?

## Acceptance

- Outside 541 mm wide, 594.4 mm high, 539 mm from the wall to the door's face. The sheet parts weigh about 34.8 kg.
- Each of the two upper wall anchors is pulled with about 204 N and each of the four anchors carries about 196 N of
  shear, with the gear in place; the form says the back plate allows a pull of 421 N at an upper fixing and limits the
  gear to about 122 kg in this cabinet (wall, 2 mm steel, this depth and height), and it says (labels, parameter
  descriptions) that the anchors and the wall must be checked by a competent person. All assertions pass.
- Bending: inner radius 2 mm, V-die 12 mm, bend allowance about 4.39 mm; the quarter-turn lock takes the cam with a
  24 mm offset (CH), the largest the catalogue has.
- Parts to cut: 13 blanks, among them 4 rails and 3 filler panels and one door.
- Rails: 465.1 mm between hole centres (measured on the model), holes square 9.5 mm, 36 holes on each rail face; the
  front opening between the side lips is more than 488 mm and the rails are 450 mm apart.
- The door hangs on its two pins at the right, the lock is on the left, and the vents are short slots with bridges
  between them, none longer than 40 mm. The back has two keyholes below and two round holes above.
- The exported flat patterns (DXF, the lid, pan, side, back, rail, door and panel, nothing to pick) hold each part's
  outline and bend lines; the door's has its vent slots, its two pin holes and the lock's double-D cut-out; the lid's
  has four glands and one pin hole.
- Refusal: 200 kg of gear cannot be set (the limit in this cabinet is about 122 kg); a 16 U cabinet on the wall cannot
  be set either (15 U at most on a wall, 24 U on the floor).
