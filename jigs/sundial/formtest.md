# Form test

We live in Leuven, at 50.88 degrees north and 4.70 degrees east, on Central European Time. The back wall of our house
does not face quite south: it turns about 25 degrees towards the east. I would like a brass sundial of 300 mm on a
slate plaque on that wall, showing the time our clocks show in winter, and I want to know how far it is off in summer and
over the year. My laser shop cuts 3 mm brass with a 0.15 mm kerf and has a fibre laser to engrave it, in one job before
the parts are cut out. Which hours will the dial show, how tall is the gnomon, what do I send to the shop, and how do they
line the engraving up with the plate? A friend's wall turns 60 degrees from south: could the same dial be made for it?

## Acceptance

- Place Custom with latitude 50.88°, longitude 4.70°, time zone +1; a vertical (wall) dial with the wall 25° east of
  south (−25 on the form), clock time, 300 mm, 3 mm brass, laser kerf 0.15 mm.
- The dial shows 12 hour lines, from 6 to 17 o'clock (clock time), with half-hour ticks between them. Longitude correction
  41.2 minutes (the sun is due south at about 12:41 CET, equation of time aside); style height 34.9°; the gnomon stands
  93 mm off the dial; numerals 16.5 mm high; a slate plaque 356 mm square; about 1.9 kg of brass.
- The answer names summer time (add an hour) and the equation of time (the description of *Time shown* gives the minutes
  per month, up to about 16 minutes either way).
- All assertions that apply pass (20 of 21; the twin noon line's is inactive).
- Export: *Cut sheet* gives one file with the dial plate (300 mm, three notches, two slots and two screw holes) and the
  gnomon with its two tabs, drawn as the beam's path for the 0.15 mm kerf, about 401 by 300 mm; *Engraving* gives one
  region 288 by 288 mm (rings, lines, ticks, numerals and three index marks that point at the plate's notches).
- Lining up: the answer gives the output *Engraving inset*, 9.1 mm (9.075): the engraving's box goes that far inside the
  plate's box from its left and bottom edges.
- Refusal: a wall 60° from south cannot be set (the form's range for this latitude stops at 50°).
