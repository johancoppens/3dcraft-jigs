# Form test: laser-box

"I keep sewing notions and want a small box for the shelf, in birch plywood I measured at 3.2 mm. My laser
burns about 0.12 mm and I will glue the joints. The room inside must be 150 mm wide, 100 mm front to back and
40 mm high. I want six compartments, three across and two from front to back. There should be a lid I lift
with my thumb at the front, not a hole in the top. Give me the sheet to send to the laser."

## Acceptance

- The form reports inside 150 × 100 × 40 mm, six compartments of about 47.9 × 48.4 mm clear, 10 parts to cut,
  about 5.1 m of cutting and about 160 g.
- The exported sheet (DXF or SVG of the set *Cut sheet*) has 10 layers: front and back, two sides, the
  bottom, the lid, its frame, one divider running across the width and two running front to back.
- The bottom panel has seven small mortises for the dividers' tabs. The lid has no hole; the front wall has a half-round notch in its top edge, and the back wall does not.
- All assertions pass.
- Asked for five compartments across, the form refuses; a finger width under twice the sheet is refused too.
