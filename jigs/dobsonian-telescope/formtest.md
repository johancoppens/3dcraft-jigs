# Form test

My father ground a 250 mm f/5 mirror, about 40 mm thick, and it has been sitting in a cupboard. I want to build the
telescope for it: a classic Dobsonian on a rocker box of 18 mm birch plywood, with a white painted cardboard form tube
because that is what I can buy here. I measured the wall of a sample at about 3 mm. I have a 2-inch Crayford focuser and I mostly use a big 2-inch wide-field eyepiece of
about half a kilo. I would like the secondary sized so a half-inch field is fully lit, and I want a red-dot finder on it.
I can get my eye up to about 1.7 metres. Show me the telescope pointed at about 45 degrees. What do I build, where do the
bearings go, how high will the eyepiece be, what is my secondary mirror, and what do I cut from the plywood?

## Acceptance

The numbers are the model's at the job above (250 mm, f/5, 40 mm, 12.7 mm field, paper tube with the 3.2 mm wall, 2-inch Crayford,
0.55 kg eyepiece, red-dot finder, four vanes, 18 mm plywood, 1.7 m eye height, altitude 45 degrees).

- Focal length 1250 mm; a 12-inch form tube (304.8 mm inside, 311.2 mm outside), about 1282 mm long.
- Secondary minor axis 58.3 mm (obstruction 23.3 % of the diameter).
- Altitude axis 432 mm from the rear of the tube and 608 mm above the ground; altitude bearings 365 mm in diameter.
- Eyepiece 846 mm above the ground at the horizon, 1276 mm at the zenith, 1317 mm at its highest: under the 1.7 m reach.
- Optical tube about 14.6 kg, the whole telescope about 27.9 kg. The balance point measured on the model is about 1.3 mm off
  the altitude axis (the output says it), inside the Jig's 4 mm tolerance. Every assertion that applies passes.
- The form says a push of about 4.8 N at the eyepiece moves the telescope in altitude and about 13.7 N in azimuth.
- The cut sheet exports as one SVG or DXF of twelve plywood parts (two side boards, front board, back board, round rocker
  floor, round ground board, two altitude bearing discs, mirror cell plate, tube rear plate, two retainers), and a saw list gives their sizes.
- Refusal: a 2-inch eyepiece on the 1.25-inch focuser cannot be entered (the eyepiece list stops at the 1.25-inch
  eyepieces), and the focal ratio cannot go above what keeps the eyepiece under 1.7 m (about f/6.3 for this mirror).
